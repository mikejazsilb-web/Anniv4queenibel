HAPPY ANNIVERSARY! ❤️

Happy Anniversary, My Love ❤️

Happy anniversary to us, my love. I’m so grateful that we made it this far together. Every moment we’ve shared our happy memories, our little arguments, our laughter, and even our difficult days means so much to me.

I also want to sincerely say I’m sorry for the things I said and for the times I may have hurt you with my words. I know that sometimes I speak without thinking, especially when I’m upset, but that doesn’t excuse it. You didn’t deserve to be hurt by the words I said. I regret those moments, and I hope you can forgive me.

I’m sorry for the times I made you feel sad, disappointed, or unappreciated. I want you to know that I never want my words or actions to make you feel like you are not important to me. You mean so much to me, and I’m still learning how to become a better partner for you.

Thank you for staying, for understanding me, and for giving me chances even when I make mistakes. I promise to try harder to listen more, think before I speak, communicate better, and treat your heart with more care.

One year together is a beautiful milestone, and I’m thankful that I get to celebrate it with you. I hope we continue making memories, supporting each other, and growing together.

I’m sorry for my mistakes, I’m thankful for your love, and I’m excited for all the memories we still have ahead of us.

Happy anniversary, my love. ❤️

I love you always. 💕

From: Ur Bana ❤️
